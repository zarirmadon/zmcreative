ZM CREATIVE is the visual practice of Zarir Madon — a constantly evolving studio working across fine art photography, digital art, graphic design and visual experimentation. The work moves freely between disciplines. Photography can become painting. Geometry can become an image. A simple idea can become an entire visual world.

05 / About

Hi.
I'm Zarir.

I'm a visual artist, photographer and designer based in Auckland, New Zealand. My work sits somewhere between photography, painting, digital experimentation and graphic design. I'm interested in images that don't quite belong to one category — photographs that feel painted, designs that behave like artworks, and ideas that become visual experiments.

ZM CREATIVE is where those different threads come together.
