03 — The idea
The photograph records the place.
The artwork records the perspective.
ZM Creative begins with photography, but doesn't end there. Colour, texture, geometry and atmosphere are pushed until the familiar becomes something new — somewhere between photograph, painting and memory.

04 — The studio
ZM

ZM Creative is an independent art and design studio built around one simple idea: perspective changes everything.

The studio turns original photography into expressive digital artworks with a strong sense of colour, architecture, atmosphere and place. The work is created for people who want something more distinctive than generic wall décor.

ZM Creative is the art/product-facing studio. The broader photographic portfolio lives separately at zarirmadon.com ↗.
